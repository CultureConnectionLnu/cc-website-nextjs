# Bylaws of the Culture Connection

## Name and Purpose

### Name of the association

The name of the organization is "**Culture Connection**".

### Associations address

**Culture Connection**

Address: Pelarplatsen 8
Post code: 352 52
Country: Sweden
City: Växjö

### Purpose of the association

Culture Connection creates affordable non-alcohol-based weekly events to bridge and strengthen the surrounding communities.

## Membership

### Membership

Culture Connection creates affordable non-alcohol-based weekly events to bridge and strengthen the surrounding communities.

### Membership Cost

- A membership is granted when the membership is confirmed or when granted honorary membership.
- **Period 1**: July-December
- **Period 2**: January-June
- Membership fees are **non-refundable**, including removal by the board.

### Membership Fee

The cost of memberships is set by the board in a member meeting prior to the beginning of each period.

### New Members

The Association shall confirm their membership. A membership card (physical or digital) serves as proof.

### Updated Membership List

The secretary shall maintain an up-to-date list of members for effective communication.

## Board of Directors

### Composition

The board of directors shall consist of at least three board members. The role of President, Treasurer, and Secretary are mandatory roles that every board must have. Culture Connection aims to have seven board members, including but not limited to:

- President
- Vice President
- Treasurer
- Secretary
- Social Media Manager
- Head of Committees
- Event Manager

### Duties and Responsibilities

When recruiting new members, sponsors, grants and collaborators Board and committee members must do so in alignment with the values of Culture Connection.

### President

- Lead the association towards its mission.
- Serve as the official spokesperson.
- Preside over meetings.
- Direct and delegate board duties.
- Act as the primary document signer.

### Vice President

- Assist the President and preside in their absence.
- Collaborate with board members to implement goals.
- Maintain sponsor relationships.

### Treasurer

- Manage finances, budgets, and records.
- Prepare transparent financial reports.
- Handle transactions, reimbursements (with approval).
- Submit grant proposals.

### Secretary

- Manage membership records.
- Document meeting minutes.
- Prepare meeting agendas.
- Oversee public relations.

### Social Media Manager

- Manage online presence and promotions.
- Maintain the website and social media accounts.

### Head of Committees

- Act as a liaison between committees and the board.
- Evaluate committee performance and streamline as needed.
- Manage and recruit new committee members as needed.

### Event Manager

- Supervise event logistics and avoid schedule conflicts.
- Manage event proposals and calendars.
- Promote events externally

### Election and Terms

- All officer roles are to be announced and open for applications for the yearly elections in March
- Officers shall be elected annually by the Election Committee and general members.
- Officers serve one-year terms and may be re-elected.
- Interim officers may be appointed in case of early departures.
- In case of an emergency (“emergency” defined by the board), a re-election of an officer role may be conducted during the autumn biannual meeting, in case such it exists.

## Committees

### Standing Committees

#### Weekly Events Committees

Separate Committees for each weekly event (e.g., climbing and board games.) General responsibilities for these Committee members include but are not limited to:

- Attending and managing assigned event
- Providing feedback to the board regarding assigned event

Committee members are allowed to attend and give propositions at board meetings but do not have voting rights.

#### Special Purpose Committees

- Appointed as needed.
- Committee members may propose but do not have voting rights.
- Examples: Election, Design and Media.

#### Election Committee

- Composed of 3 members.
- Members cannot be current board members.

### Volunteer

For special events and other needs, a temporary unofficial “Volunteer role” can be assigned to members. Members with this role do not have the responsibilities or the benefits of a Committee member, such as attending board meetings.

## Meetings

### Bi-annual Member Meeting

- Every person present whose membership is confirmed before the start of the annual member meeting can vote.
- The individuals elected as vote counters during the annual member meeting cannot vote.
- Proposals and the elections of officers are accepted with a majority vote at the bi-annual member meeting

Held **twice a year**:

- **Period 1**: October
- **Period 2**: March

### Purpose:

- Elect officers (Only in March)
- Receive Treasury reports.
- Recap the association’s progress.
- Address additional issues.

### Notification

Announce meetings 1 month in advance via social media or print.

### Regular Meetings

- Held at least once a month during the academic year at a time determined by the board.
- Board members must be present (whether physically or digitally) to vote.

### Emergency Board Meetings

- Can be called by any board member.
- **Notice:** 1 business day.

## Finances

### Fiscal Year

The fiscal year runs from 1st of April to 31st of March.

### Budget and Financial Records

The treasurer prepares the budget, which must be approved at the bi-annual meeting in March.

## Event Policies

### Attendance and Conduct

- The board holds the right to **dismiss attendees**.
- Discriminatory behavior or harassment is not tolerated.

## Amendments

### Proposal

Proposed amendments must be submitted in writing 2 weeks before the member meeting.

### Adoption

Amendments are adopted by a **majority vote** at the annual meeting.

Changes to the bylaws that only concern typos and clarifications that do not alter meaning can be approved by the board at regular meetings.

## Dissolution

The dissolution, pausing, or the change of mission or values of the association requires a vote by the founding board members according to the first Biannual meeting.

**Remaining assets** shall be distributed to a non-profit organization with a similar purpose, as determined by the board.
